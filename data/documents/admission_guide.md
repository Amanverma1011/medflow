# Admission Guide

*Synthetic demo document. MedFlow General Hospital is fictional.*

## Documents needed for admission

For admission you need a government-issued photo ID, your insurance card or policy details, the admission letter or referral from your doctor, and a list of your current medication. If you have them, also bring previous discharge summaries and recent test results.

A parent or legal guardian must accompany patients under 18 and bring the child's birth certificate or ID.

## The admission process

Go to the Admissions desk on the Ground Floor of the Main Building at the time stated in your admission letter. Staff will confirm your details, explain the consent forms, and take you to your ward. A nurse will then complete an assessment, including your medical history and allergies.

## Planned admissions

For a planned procedure, follow the fasting and medication instructions in your admission letter. Arrange for someone to take you home afterwards if you are having sedation or an anaesthetic.

## Emergency admissions

Patients admitted through the Emergency Department can complete the paperwork later. A family member can bring the required documents within 24 hours.

## Deposits and insurance

The billing team will explain any deposit and confirm your insurance cover before a planned admission. Bring your insurer's pre-authorisation reference if you have one.

## Your room and belongings

Each bed has a lockable bedside cabinet. The hospital cannot accept responsibility for valuables kept on the ward.

## Going home

Discharge is usually completed by 11:00 AM. Your nurse will go through your discharge summary and medication before you leave.
