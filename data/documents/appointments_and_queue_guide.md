# Appointments and Queue Guide

*Synthetic demo document. MedFlow General Hospital is fictional.*

## How to book an appointment

You can book an appointment in the patient portal. Open the Appointments page, choose Book appointment, select a department and doctor, and pick an available time slot. You will receive a confirmation notification straight away.

You can also book by speaking to the reception desk in any outpatient department.

## How to reschedule or cancel an appointment

Open the Appointments page in the patient portal, select the appointment, and choose Reschedule or Cancel. Please give at least 24 hours' notice where possible so the slot can be offered to another patient.

## Appointment reminders

Reminders are sent through the patient portal on the day before and on the morning of your appointment.

## Checking in

When you arrive, check in from the Queue page of the patient portal or at the department reception desk. Check-in opens on the day of your appointment. You will be given a queue token, such as A-047.

## How the digital queue works

After check-in, the Queue page shows your token, the token currently being served, the number of patients ahead of you, and an estimated waiting time. The estimate updates automatically as the queue moves. You will receive a notification when you are next and when it is your turn.

Estimated waiting times are predictions and can change if an urgent case needs to be seen first.

## If you miss your turn

If you are not present when your token is called, you will be moved back in the queue rather than losing your place completely. Speak to reception if you need help.

## Arriving late

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, reception will try to fit you in, but you may need to wait longer or rebook.

## What to bring to an appointment

Bring your photo ID, insurance details, your medication list and any previous test results or referral letters.
