# Billing and Insurance Guide

*Synthetic demo document. MedFlow General Hospital is fictional.*

## Understanding your bill

Your bill lists each consultation, test, procedure and medication separately. Outpatient bills are issued at the end of the visit. Inpatient bills are issued at discharge, with an itemised statement available on request.

## How to pay

You can pay at the billing counter on the Ground Floor of the Main Building, at self-service kiosks in each outpatient department, or online through the patient portal. Cards and bank transfers are accepted.

## Using insurance

Show your insurance card at registration. For planned admissions and some scans, your insurer may need to approve the treatment in advance. The billing team can help you request pre-authorisation.

## Estimates

You can ask the billing team for a written cost estimate before a planned procedure. The final bill can differ if your treatment changes.

## Billing questions

If you think there is a mistake on your bill, contact the billing office with your bill number. Queries are reviewed within five working days.

## Financial assistance

Patients who have difficulty paying can ask the billing office about payment plans.

## Billing office hours

The billing office is open from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays and from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturdays.
