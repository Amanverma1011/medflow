# Cholesterol Guide

*Synthetic demo document for MedFlow General Hospital. Educational content only.*

## What is cholesterol

Cholesterol is a fatty substance made by the liver and found in some foods. The body needs some cholesterol to build cells, but too much in the blood can build up in the walls of the arteries and raise the risk of heart disease and stroke.

## Types of cholesterol

LDL cholesterol is often called bad cholesterol because high levels contribute to fatty deposits in the arteries. HDL cholesterol is often called good cholesterol because it helps carry cholesterol away from the arteries. Triglycerides are another type of blood fat measured in the same test.

## The cholesterol test

A cholesterol test, also called a lipid panel, is a blood test. Some lipid tests require fasting, and your request form will tell you if yours does. Your doctor interprets the result alongside your age, blood pressure, smoking status and family history rather than on its own.

## High cholesterol

High cholesterol does not cause symptoms. It is found through a blood test. It can run in families and is also affected by diet, weight, activity and alcohol.

## Lowering cholesterol

Eating less saturated fat, eating more fibre, being active and stopping smoking can all lower cholesterol. Some people are also prescribed medication such as a statin. Whether medication is right for you is a decision to make with your doctor.

## When to seek medical care

Ask your doctor about a cholesterol check if you have a family history of early heart disease or have never been tested.
