# Department Directory and Wayfinding

*Synthetic demo document. MedFlow General Hospital is fictional.*

## Where is the radiology department

The Radiology department is on Level B1 of the Main Building. Take the main lifts from the lobby down one floor and follow the blue signs. X-ray, CT, MRI and ultrasound are all in this department. Radiology reception is open from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

## Where is the emergency department

The Emergency Department is on the Ground Floor of the Main Building and has its own 24-hour entrance on the north side, marked with red signs. It is open at all hours, every day.

## Where is cardiology

The Cardiology department is in the East Wing on Level 2. From the Main Building lobby, follow the green signs along the link corridor to the East Wing lifts.

## Where is neurology

The Neurology department is in the East Wing on Level 3, one floor above Cardiology.

## Where is orthopedics

The Orthopedics department is in the West Wing on Level 1. The fracture clinic and physiotherapy gym are next to it.

## Where is pediatrics

The Pediatrics department is on Level 1 of the Children's Pavilion, which has its own entrance and a play area in the waiting room.

## Where is general medicine

General Medicine outpatient clinics are on the Ground Floor of the Main Building, to the left of the main reception desk.

## Where is oncology

The Oncology department and day treatment unit are in the East Wing on Level 4.

## Laboratory and pharmacy

The laboratory collection centre and the outpatient pharmacy are side by side on the Ground Floor of the Main Building.

## Getting help finding your way

Volunteers in green jackets at the main reception desk can walk you to your department. Wheelchairs are available at every entrance.
