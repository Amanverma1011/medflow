# Discharge Instructions

*Synthetic demo document for MedFlow General Hospital. Educational content only.*

## Before you leave

Before discharge, your nurse will go through your discharge summary with you. Make sure you understand your diagnosis, any new medication, follow-up appointments, and who to contact with questions. Ask for anything unclear to be explained again.

## Your medication

You will receive a list of your medication, including any changes made during your stay. Take medication as prescribed and do not restart old medication that was stopped in hospital unless your doctor tells you to.

## Wound care

Keep the wound clean and dry. Follow the instructions you were given about changing dressings and when you can shower. Some redness and mild soreness around a wound is normal in the first few days.

## Activity and rest

Rest is an important part of recovery, but gentle movement helps prevent blood clots and stiffness. Increase activity gradually and follow any specific limits you were given about lifting, driving or returning to work.

## Eating and drinking

Unless you were given other advice, return to your normal diet and drink plenty of fluids.

## Follow-up appointments

Your follow-up appointment details are on your discharge summary. You can view, reschedule or cancel appointments in the patient portal.

## When to seek medical care

Contact the ward or your doctor if you have increasing pain, a fever, a wound that is red, swollen or leaking, or persistent vomiting. Call your local emergency service immediately for chest pain, difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, or a painful swollen calf.
