# Emergency Department Instructions

*Synthetic demo document. MedFlow General Hospital is fictional.*

## When to come to the Emergency Department

Come to the Emergency Department, or call your local emergency service, for serious or life-threatening problems. These include chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of a stroke, severe bleeding, major injuries, loss of consciousness and severe allergic reactions.

## How triage works

Every patient is assessed by a triage nurse on arrival. Triage sorts patients by how urgent their condition is, not by arrival time. Patients with the most serious conditions are seen first, so someone who arrives after you may be seen before you.

## Waiting times

Waiting times in the Emergency Department vary through the day and are usually longest in the early evening. Tell the triage nurse straight away if your symptoms get worse while you wait.

## What to bring

If possible, bring your photo ID, a list of your medication, and details of any allergies. Do not delay coming to hospital to look for documents.

## Eating and drinking

Please check with a nurse before eating or drinking, because some tests and procedures require an empty stomach.

## Companions

One companion may stay with each patient. A parent or guardian may stay with a child at all times.

## After your visit

You will be given written advice and any prescriptions before you leave. A summary of your visit is sent to your regular doctor and added to your patient portal.
