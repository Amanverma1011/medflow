# Heart-Healthy Living Guide

*Synthetic demo document for MedFlow General Hospital. Educational content only.*

## Eating well

A heart-healthy diet is based on vegetables, fruit, whole grains, beans, nuts and fish. Choose unsaturated fats such as olive oil instead of butter or fried food. Limit salt, sugary drinks, and processed meat. Reading food labels helps you compare salt and sugar content.

## Physical activity

Adults are encouraged to be active most days of the week. Brisk walking, cycling and swimming all count. Start slowly and build up gradually. If you have a heart condition or have been inactive for a long time, ask your doctor what level of activity is right for you before starting.

## Healthy weight

Keeping to a healthy weight reduces strain on the heart and lowers the risk of high blood pressure and type 2 diabetes. Small, steady changes are easier to maintain than strict diets.

## Smoking and alcohol

Stopping smoking is one of the most effective ways to protect your heart, and the benefit starts within weeks. The hospital's stop-smoking service offers free support. Keep alcohol within recommended limits and have several alcohol-free days each week.

## Sleep and stress

Poor sleep and long-term stress can raise blood pressure. Aim for a regular sleep routine and make time for activities that help you relax.

## Regular checks

Have your blood pressure, cholesterol and blood glucose checked as often as your doctor recommends. Knowing your numbers helps you and your care team act early.
