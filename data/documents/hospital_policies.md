# Hospital Policies

*Synthetic demo document. MedFlow General Hospital is fictional.*

## Privacy and confidentiality

Your health information is confidential. It is shared only with the staff involved in your care and is accessed on a need-to-know basis. Access to patient records is logged and audited.

## Requesting your medical records

You can request a copy of your medical records from the Health Records office on the Ground Floor of the Main Building, or through the Documents page of the patient portal. Bring photo ID. Requests are normally completed within 10 working days.

## Consent

Staff will explain any proposed test or treatment, including its benefits and risks, and ask for your consent before it goes ahead. You may ask questions, ask for more time, or change your mind.

## Smoke-free site

Smoking and vaping are not allowed anywhere on the hospital site, including car parks and entrances.

## Respectful behaviour

Patients, visitors and staff are expected to treat each other with respect. Abusive or violent behaviour is not tolerated.

## Photography and recording

Please do not photograph or record other patients, visitors or staff without their permission.

## Mobile phones

Mobile phones may be used in most areas. Please keep calls short and use silent mode on wards.

## Complaints and compliments

You can raise a concern with the nurse in charge, through the patient portal, or with the Patient Experience office. Complaints are acknowledged within two working days.

## Interpreters

Free interpreter services are available. Tell reception or your nurse which language you need.
