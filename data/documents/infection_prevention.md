# Infection Prevention Guide

*Synthetic demo document for MedFlow General Hospital. Educational content only.*

## Hand hygiene

Clean hands are the most effective way to prevent the spread of infection. Wash your hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds, or use alcohol hand rub, before eating, after using the toilet, and when entering or leaving a ward. Hand rub dispensers are at every ward entrance and bedside.

You are welcome to ask any member of staff whether they have cleaned their hands before they care for you.

## Visitors

Visitors should not come to the hospital if they have a cough, fever, vomiting or diarrhoea. Visitors should clean their hands on arrival and avoid sitting on patient beds.

## Coughs and sneezes

Cover your mouth and nose with a tissue or your elbow when you cough or sneeze. Put used tissues in a bin and clean your hands afterwards. Masks are available at reception.

## Wounds and devices

Do not touch wounds, dressings, drips or catheters. Tell a nurse if a dressing becomes loose or wet, or if the area around a drip becomes red, swollen or painful.

## Isolation rooms

Some patients are cared for in single rooms to prevent infection spreading. Please follow the instructions on the door and speak to a nurse before entering.

## When to seek medical care

After leaving hospital, contact your doctor if a wound becomes red, hot, swollen or starts to leak, or if you develop a fever.
