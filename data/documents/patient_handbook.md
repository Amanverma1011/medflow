# Hospital Patient Handbook

*Synthetic demo document. MedFlow General Hospital is fictional.*

## Visiting hours

General ward visiting hours are 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM and 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM every day. Two visitors may be at the bedside at one time. The Intensive Care Unit allows one visitor at a time between 11:00 AM and 12:00 PM and between 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM. The Pediatric Ward welcomes one parent or guardian at any hour, including overnight.

Visitors who feel unwell are asked not to visit.

## What to bring for a hospital stay

Bring your photo ID, insurance details, a list of your current medication, comfortable clothing, toiletries and any mobility or hearing aids you use. Leave valuables and large amounts of cash at home.

## Meals

Patient meals are served at 8:00 AM, 12:30 PM and 6:30 PM. Tell your nurse about any dietary needs or allergies. The cafeteria on the Ground Floor of the Main Building is open to visitors from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

## Parking

Visitor parking is in the multi-storey car park opposite the Main Building entrance. The first 30 minutes are free. Accessible parking bays are on Level 1 of the car park, next to the lift.

## Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi is available across the hospital. Connect to the network named MedFlow-Guest and accept the terms of use.

## Pharmacy

The outpatient pharmacy is on the Ground Floor of the Main Building, next to the laboratory collection centre. It is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM at weekends.

## Lost property

Lost property is held at the security desk in the Main Building lobby for 30 days.

## Giving feedback

You can share feedback in the patient portal after any visit, or speak to the nurse in charge. Feedback is reviewed by the Patient Experience team every week.
