# Sleep and Mental Wellbeing Guide

*Synthetic demo document for MedFlow General Hospital. Educational content only.*

## Why sleep matters

Sleep supports memory, mood, the immune system and heart health. Most adults need seven to nine hours of sleep a night. Ongoing poor sleep is linked with higher blood pressure and low mood.

## Tips for better sleep

Go to bed and wake up at regular times. Keep the bedroom dark, quiet and cool. Avoid caffeine in the afternoon and evening, and avoid screens for an hour before bed. Regular daytime activity helps, but avoid vigorous exercise late in the evening.

## Managing stress

Stress is a normal response to pressure, but long-term stress can affect health. Helpful approaches include regular physical activity, breathing exercises, time outdoors, keeping in touch with friends and family, and breaking large problems into smaller steps.

## Anxiety and low mood

Feeling anxious or low from time to time is common. Talk to your doctor if these feelings last more than two weeks, are getting worse, or affect your work, relationships or sleep. Effective support and treatment are available.

## Supporting someone else

Listen without judgement, ask how you can help, and encourage the person to speak to a healthcare professional. You do not need to have all the answers.

## When to seek medical care

If you or someone else is thinking about self-harm or is at immediate risk, contact your local emergency service or go to the nearest Emergency Department. Help is available at any hour.
