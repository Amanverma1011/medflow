# When to Seek Urgent or Emergency Care

*Synthetic demo document for MedFlow General Hospital. Educational content only.*

## Emergency symptoms

Seek emergency care immediately for symptoms that may be life-threatening. These include chest pain or pressure, difficulty breathing, signs of a stroke such as face drooping, arm weakness or slurred speech, severe bleeding that will not stop, loss of consciousness, a seizure, a severe allergic reaction, or a serious head injury.

Call your local emergency service rather than travelling by yourself. Do not wait to see whether the symptoms pass.

## When to seek urgent care

You should seek urgent care the same day for problems that are not life-threatening but should not wait. Examples include a high fever that does not settle, a cut that may need stitches, a suspected broken bone, persistent vomiting or diarrhoea with signs of dehydration, a painful urinary infection, or a sudden worsening of a long-term condition.

Urgent care is appropriate when you cannot wait for a routine appointment but your life is not in immediate danger.

## When a routine appointment is appropriate

Book a routine appointment for ongoing or mild symptoms, medication reviews, repeat prescriptions, test results and general health checks.

## Children and older adults

Babies, young children and older adults can become unwell quickly. Seek urgent advice for a baby under three months with a fever, a child who is unusually drowsy or not drinking, or an older adult with new confusion.

## Mental health emergencies

If you or someone else is at immediate risk of harm, contact your local emergency service or go to the nearest Emergency Department. Support is available at any hour.

## What to bring

Bring a list of your medication, details of allergies, and any relevant medical documents. If possible, bring someone with you.

## If you are unsure

If you are unsure how serious a problem is, it is safer to seek medical advice than to wait. This guide does not replace assessment by a qualified healthcare professional.
